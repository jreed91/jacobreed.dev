## Overview

The conversation around Azure DevOps and GitHub goes beyond features. It raises a broader question: which platform best supports the kind of engineering organization you’re trying to build.

As Microsoft continues expanding AI-enabled development capabilities within GitHub, the difference between the two platforms is widening. For many organizations, this raises important questions about the downstream impact platform decisions will have on developer experience, talent attraction and retention, delivery performance, and long-term innovation.
