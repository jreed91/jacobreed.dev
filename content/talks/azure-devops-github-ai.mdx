## Overview

Modernization often stalls not because teams don’t know where they need to go — but because getting there feels heavy, risky, and slow. CI/CD is a perfect example. Legacy pipelines linger, teams work around brittle systems, and even “simple” platform shifts can consume weeks of effort. Sound familiar? The pressure to modernize is real, but so is the fear of disrupting delivery while doing it.
