## What it is

Stride generates a training plan from your goal race and then keeps it honest as life
happens. Most plans are a PDF that stops being true the first week you miss a session;
this one treats the plan as something that gets rebuilt rather than abandoned.

## The plan, and what happens when you miss it

`TrainingPlanService` builds the schedule from your goal, fitness level and available
equipment. From there a set of services exist specifically to handle the plan going wrong:
`MissedWorkoutDetector` and `MissedWorkoutShift` notice what you skipped,
`WorkoutReorderPlanner` and `PlanRebuildWindow` decide what can move and how far, and
`AdaptableWorkoutSelector` picks the substitute. The plan absorbs the miss instead of
leaving you behind it.

## Running

Live tracking covers pace, splits and heart-rate zones, with a voice coach
(`RunVoiceCoach`) announcing splits as you go, lap recording, and pace alerts when you
drift off the prescription. A Live Activity puts the run on the Lock Screen and in the
Dynamic Island; Home Screen widgets carry the next session.

**Route Genius** generates new routes from wherever you are standing, through the
OpenRouteService Directions API. The API token is read from the device Keychain rather
than shipped in the bundle — you paste it once in Settings and it is stored
`AfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly`, never in `UserDefaults` and never synced through iCloud.

## Coaching that knows your context

The coach layer reads your recent training rather than reciting generic advice.
`CoachSituation`, `CoachBaseline` and `SmartCoachCards` assemble what is worth saying
right now, ranked — a primary card with a derivation line showing where its numbers came
from, and supporting cards beneath it.

Two policies exist because the standard advice is actively wrong for the people they
cover, and both are backed by evidence write-ups in the repo:

- **`HeatAdaptationPolicy`** — how much to pull back, and for how long, when the weather
  turns. Paired with `HeatAdvisoryService` and a WeatherKit forecast.
- **`PregnancyRunningPolicy`** and `PregnancyPlanService` — plan generation for running
  through pregnancy.

## Under the hood

Workouts sync through HealthKit and CloudKit, `AppContainer` is the composition root for
every repository and service, and the Xcode project is generated from `project.yml` with
XcodeGen rather than committed — so there is no `.pbxproj` to conflict on. CI lints with
SwiftLint, builds, and gates on tests; Xcode Cloud handles archive and TestFlight builds,
assembling release notes from commit history.

There is also a UI-test target, `UXCaptureTests`, that drives the app and captures every
screen with seeded demo data — which is how the app's UX has been reviewed from what
actually lands on the glass rather than from reading source.
