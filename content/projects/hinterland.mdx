## The problem

Hinterland Music Festival puts 15,000 people in a rural valley in St. Charles, Iowa.
Cell service there is unusable — not slow, unusable — which makes a festival app that
fetches its schedule the one kind of app that cannot work at the moment you need it.

So this one assumes there is no network at all.

## Everything ships in the binary

The full four-day schedule, every artist bio, all 48 pieces of artist artwork, the
festival's four maps, the vendor directory and eleven years of past lineups are bundled
into the app. All of it works in airplane mode.

The network is only ever used to enrich what is already on the phone — set-time changes,
the forecast, crowd ratings, and each artist's top songs — and every one of those caches
what it fetches and falls back to what it already had.

## Building your weekend

- **Schedule** — four days across the Main, Miniland and Campfire stages, filterable by
  stage, with a card at the top showing what is on now or up next.
- **My Lineup** — star sets to build a personal schedule, with automatic detection of
  overlapping sets so you can see what you are choosing between.
- **Reminders** — local notifications 5–60 minutes before your starred sets, scheduled
  on-device so they fire with no signal.
- **Widget and Live Activity** — "Up Next" on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, and the set
  you are watching in the Dynamic Island from 90 minutes out, counting down on its own.

## The rest of the grounds

**Food & Drink** covers all 33 stands, grouped by where they are actually parked — East,
West and South Concourse, VIP, GA+, Basecamp and the mobile carts — with combinable
filters for vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free and sugar-free.

**Maps** bundles the grounds, concourse, driving and shuttle-parking maps as pinch-zoomable
artwork, plus a MapKit view that georeferences the grounds map so the blue dot shows where
you are standing on it.

**Weather** pulls a WeatherKit forecast for the amphitheater itself. Severe and extreme
National Weather Service advisories break out onto the top of the schedule, and each set on
an artist's page shows what the weather will be doing while they play.

**Past lineups** goes back to 2015 — every headliner and every full bill, day by day, side
stages marked. Bundled, so it settles the argument about who played 2017 with no signal.

## Rating sets, and the recap

Any set can be rated one to five stars once it is under way, with an optional line about
it. Rating works with no signal and uploads later; scores are pooled through CloudKit so
every set also carries the average everyone else gave it. Your notes stay on your phone.

Once the last set ends, the Schedule tab becomes a wrap-up of the weekend: how many sets
you saw and for how many hours, your best of the festival, the day you gave the most to,
and the festival's best sets according to everyone who rated one — shareable as a rendered
card. Then the year joins the archive.
